Bandcamp

Nemoviz BC Finder is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Bandcamp.
Bandcamp is a trademark of its owner. The music, artwork and texts belong to their
artists and labels.

What the program uses, and nothing else:
  * Bandcamp Discover (bandcamp.com/api/discover/1/discover_web), the same service
    bandcamp.com/discover uses, and the one /api/ address Bandcamp's robots.txt allows;
  * the Bandcamp embedded player (bandcamp.com/EmbeddedPlayer), for listening;
  * links to release and artist pages, opened in your own browser.
It does not read release pages or use Bandcamp search, streams or downloads outside the player, and it asks
at most once every 1.5 seconds.

The genre and subgenre list (podaci\zanrovi.txt) is taken from bandcamp.com/discover.

Bandcamp's terms of use apply to everything heard and opened through it:
https://bandcamp.com/terms_of_use
